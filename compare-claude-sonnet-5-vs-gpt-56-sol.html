Claude Sonnet 5 vs GPT-5.6 Sol: Real API Cost Compared

By Meng Guoying (mengg) · Review method · Corrections

The verdict, in numbers: On our reference 200-step agent workload, Claude Sonnet 5 bills $2.69 against $5.38 for GPT-5.6 Sol — 2.0x apart. List rates alone would not have told you that.

Engineering trade-offs & production reality

This is the battle for default production workhorse. Sonnet 5 charges a flat, permanent $2/$10 rate, whereas Sol charges a promotional $4/$20 rate (2x higher). On a 200-step task, Sonnet 5 costs roughly $2.74 versus $5.38 on Sol. Sol's advantage lies in its tight integration with OpenAI's Responses API and near-zero tool-argument schema validation errors. If your team writes custom prompt parsers, Sonnet 5 is far cheaper; if you rely heavily on complex nested JSON schemas, Sol may save engineering debugging hours.

Frequently asked questions

Is Claude Sonnet 5 cheaper than GPT-5.6 Sol?

On list rates, Claude Sonnet 5 costs $2.00/$10.00 per 1M input/output tokens versus $4.00/$20.00 for GPT-5.6 Sol. On a 200-step agent workload (40K cached prefix, 500 output tokens per step), Claude Sonnet 5 totals $2.69 versus $5.38 for GPT-5.6 Sol. Prices verified November 2026.

Claude Sonnet 5 vs GPT-5.6 Sol: which has the longer context window?

Claude Sonnet 5 offers 1M tokens; GPT-5.6 Sol offers 1.05M tokens, per provider documentation checked November 2026.

Where do these Claude Sonnet 5 and GPT-5.6 Sol prices come from?

Both rate cards were read from the providers' own pricing pages in November 2026: https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/pricing and https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models/gpt-5.6-sol.
