About AI Agent Hub

AI Agent Hub is an independently operated reference site for developers comparing AI model APIs and agent workflows. Its useful core is intentionally narrow: source-linked model data, transparent calculators, and practical explanations.

What we publish

We publish provider pricing snapshots, context limits, clearly attributed benchmark tables, calculators, and reviewed technical guides. We do not claim that a price snapshot predicts every reader's monthly bill, and we do not present vendor benchmark numbers as tests run by this site.

How the price data is verified

A pricing table is only worth publishing if a reader can reproduce it, so the catalogue follows a fixed procedure rather than being assembled from memory or from third-party trackers.

  1. Read the provider's own documentation. Announcement posts, aggregator sites and price-tracking services are not accepted as sources.
  2. Where two of a provider's own pages disagree, follow the pricing page. This is not a hypothetical rule: Anthropic's Sonnet 5 announcement still carried a "through August 31" introductory note after its pricing page had confirmed the scheduled increase would not occur. The billing page is authoritative.
  3. Record the rates that actually move a bill, not just the headline pair. Cache-read rates, long-context multipliers and cache-write charges are captured alongside input and output, because the headline pair alone produces wrong budgets.
  4. Re-check on a schedule and publish what changed. Prices drift; a catalogue that is not re-read is a liability rather than an asset.

How much drift matters is not theoretical. The 18 September 2026 re-check against provider documentation found nine of the ten catalogue entries out of date, including one rate overstated by a factor of five.

Editorial process

  1. Collect: start with provider documentation or an original project specification.
  2. Record: save the source, verification date, units, temporary-price conditions, and known caveats.
  3. Review: check calculations, links, page rendering, and whether a claim says more than its source supports.
  4. Correct: investigate documented correction reports and update the date when a data snapshot changes.

AI tools may assist with research organization, drafting, coding, or quality checks. Publication decisions, source selection, and corrections remain the responsibility of the site operator. See the detailed methodology and AI-use disclosure.

AI Agent Hub is not an official publication of Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, DeepSeek, or other covered providers. The site may display advertising, including Google AdSense if approved. Scope limits

This site does not provide legal, security, procurement, or financial guarantees. Provider terms, regional availability, taxes, rate tiers, and prices can change. Readers should confirm material decisions on the linked official source.
