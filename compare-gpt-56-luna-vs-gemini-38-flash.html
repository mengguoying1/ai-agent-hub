GPT-5.6 Luna vs Gemini 3.8 Flash: Real API Cost Compared

By Meng Guoying (mengg) · Review method · Corrections

The verdict, in numbers: On our reference 200-step agent workload, GPT-5.6 Luna bills $0.29 against $0.97 for Gemini 3.8 Flash — 3.4x apart. List rates alone would not have told you that.

Engineering trade-offs & production reality

Sub-dollar efficiency: Luna ($0.20/$1.20) vs Flash ($0.75/$3.75). Luna is 3.75x cheaper on input and 3x cheaper on output, making it one of the most cost-effective hosted models in existence. However, Gemini 3.8 Flash includes native thinking tokens and 64K output support, solving complex logical hurdles where Luna can cut corners. Luna is ideal for high-frequency linting and summarization; Flash is superior for autonomous step execution.

Frequently asked questions

Is GPT-5.6 Luna cheaper than Gemini 3.8 Flash?

On list rates, GPT-5.6 Luna costs $0.20/$1.20 per 1M input/output tokens versus $0.75/$3.75 for Gemini 3.8 Flash. On a 200-step agent workload (40K cached prefix, 500 output tokens per step), GPT-5.6 Luna totals $0.29 versus $0.97 for Gemini 3.8 Flash. Prices verified November 2026.

GPT-5.6 Luna vs Gemini 3.8 Flash: which has the longer context window?

GPT-5.6 Luna offers 1.05M tokens; Gemini 3.8 Flash offers 1M tokens, per provider documentation checked November 2026.

Where do these GPT-5.6 Luna and Gemini 3.8 Flash prices come from?

Both rate cards were read from the providers' own pricing pages in November 2026: https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models/gpt-5.6-luna and https://deepmind.google/models/gemini/flash/.
