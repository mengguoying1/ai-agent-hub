Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: August 5, 2026.

This policy describes information handling for www.waiagent.win. It is written to reflect the site's current implementation, not features that do not exist.

Information you choose to send

The site does not currently operate an account system, comments, newsletter, or web contact form. If you email the address on the contact page, your email provider and ours process your address, message, and attachments. We use that information to answer the message, handle corrections, protect the site, or maintain necessary records.

Information processed automatically

The site is hosted through GitHub Pages. Like most hosting services, GitHub may process technical request data such as IP address, browser information, requested URL, timestamps, and security events. Review the GitHub General Privacy Statement. AI Agent Hub does not currently include a separate first-party analytics package in its published code.

Advertising and Google

Selected substantive pages include the Google AdSense site code while the site applies for or uses Google advertising. Ads may not appear on every visit or page. Google may use cookies, local storage, IP addresses, device information, and activity data to provide, secure, measure, and personalize advertising subject to applicable choices and law.

  • Read How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.
  • Manage Google ad personalization at My Ad Center.
  • Review Google's Privacy Policy.

Where a certified consent platform is legally required for Google advertising, it must be enabled before personalized advertising is served. Consent-message configuration is managed through the advertising platform and may vary by region. Browser controls can also block or delete cookies.

Interactive tools

The cost calculator and token counter run in your browser. Text entered into the token counter is processed locally by page JavaScript and is not submitted by this site to an AI model or site-owned server. Do not paste secrets into any website unless you are comfortable with the device and browser environment.

External links

Links to model providers and documentation open third-party sites with their own policies. AI Agent Hub does not control their data practices.

Retention and rights

Email is kept only as long as reasonably needed to answer, document a correction, resolve abuse, or meet legal obligations. Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or objection. Requests should identify the relevant message or data and be sent to onenter608@outlook.com.

Children and security

The site is intended for a general developer audience and is not directed to children under 13. We use reasonable safeguards but cannot guarantee absolute security of internet transmission or third-party infrastructure.

Changes

Material changes will update the date above. Questions or privacy requests can be sent through the contact details.
