OpenAI GPT-6 Astra and the corrected GPT-5.6 rates

Astra joined on September 3 at $10/$50. Re-reading the model pages also corrected Sol to $4/$20, Terra to $2/$12, and Luna to $0.20/$1.20 — the previous values came from the launch post, and Luna differed by five times. OpenAI source ↗

Rechecked Sep 18, 2026

Claude Sonnet 5 price status

Anthropic cancelled the September 1 increase. Its pricing page now states that the $2/$10 rate "is now the standard price" and that the scheduled move to $3/$15 "will not occur". The row is no longer flagged. Anthropic source ↗

Rechecked Sep 18, 2026

DeepSeek V4.1 Flash and V4 Pro

Rates corrected to the off-peak card: Flash is $0.15/$0.60 and Pro $0.66/$1.98, each doubling during peak hours. DeepSeek also extended V4 Pro past its planned September 14 withdrawal. DeepSeek source ↗

Rechecked Sep 18, 2026

Gemini 3.8 Flash
