GPT-6 Luna vs Claude Haiku 5.5: Real API Cost Compared

By Meng Guoying (mengg) · Review method · Corrections

The verdict, in numbers: GPT-6 Luna and Claude Haiku 5.5 publish identical list rates ($0.10 / $0.50), but identical list rates do not mean identical bills: on our 200-step agent workload the two land 1.0x apart ($0.13 vs $0.13), driven entirely by the cache-read row.

Engineering trade-offs & production reality

Two ultra-budget champions tied at $0.10/$0.50 and $0.01/1M cache reads. Note Haiku 5.5's 100K prompt threshold (jumping to $0.50/$2.50 >100K) versus GPT-6 Luna's 272K threshold, making prompt size the deciding factor for large triage pipelines.

Frequently asked questions

Is GPT-6 Luna cheaper than Claude Haiku 5.5?

On list rates, GPT-6 Luna costs $0.10/$0.50 per 1M input/output tokens versus $0.10/$0.50 for Claude Haiku 5.5. On a 200-step agent workload (40K cached prefix, 500 output tokens per step), GPT-6 Luna totals $0.13 versus $0.13 for Claude Haiku 5.5. Prices verified November 2026.

GPT-6 Luna vs Claude Haiku 5.5: which has the longer context window?

GPT-6 Luna offers 1.05M tokens; Claude Haiku 5.5 offers 1M tokens, per provider documentation checked November 2026.

Where do these GPT-6 Luna and Claude Haiku 5.5 prices come from?

Both rate cards were read from the providers' own pricing pages in November 2026: https://openai.com/api/pricing/ and https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/pricing.
