Gemini 3.1 Pro vs GPT-5.6 Terra: Real API Cost Compared

By Meng Guoying (mengg) · Review method · Corrections

The verdict, in numbers: Gemini 3.1 Pro and GPT-5.6 Terra publish identical list rates ($2.00 / $12.00), but identical list rates do not mean identical bills: on our 200-step agent workload the two land 1.0x apart ($2.79 vs $2.89), driven entirely by the cache-read row.

Engineering trade-offs & production reality

Mid-tier showdown: Gemini 3.1 Pro ($2/$12) vs GPT-5.6 Terra ($2/$12). Both share identical input rates, but their caching mechanics differ completely. Google bills cache storage per hour with zero write fee, making Gemini 3.1 Pro remarkably cheap for bursts of short agent runs. Terra charges a 1.25x write multiplier on the first step. For multimodal input (diagrams, architecture charts, screenshots), Gemini has the edge; for pure code synthesis in GitHub Actions, Terra delivers predictable step latencies.

Frequently asked questions

Is Gemini 3.1 Pro cheaper than GPT-5.6 Terra?

On list rates, Gemini 3.1 Pro costs $2.00/$12.00 per 1M input/output tokens versus $2.00/$12.00 for GPT-5.6 Terra. On a 200-step agent workload (40K cached prefix, 500 output tokens per step), Gemini 3.1 Pro totals $2.79 versus $2.89 for GPT-5.6 Terra. Prices verified November 2026.

Gemini 3.1 Pro vs GPT-5.6 Terra: which has the longer context window?

Gemini 3.1 Pro offers 1M tokens; GPT-5.6 Terra offers 1.05M tokens, per provider documentation checked November 2026.

Where do these Gemini 3.1 Pro and GPT-5.6 Terra prices come from?
