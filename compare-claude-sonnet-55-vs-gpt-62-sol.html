Claude Sonnet 5.5 vs GPT-6.2 Sol: Real API Cost Compared

By Meng Guoying (mengg) · Review method · Corrections

The verdict, in numbers: Claude Sonnet 5.5 and GPT-6.2 Sol publish identical list rates ($2.00 / $10.00), but identical list rates do not mean identical bills: on our 200-step agent workload the two land 1.0x apart ($1.90 vs $1.90), driven entirely by the cache-read row.

Engineering trade-offs & production reality

The $2/$10 production workhorse showdown: both Sonnet 5.5 and GPT-6.2 Sol now sit at the exact same $2/$10 list rate and $0.10 cache read rate. Sonnet 5.5 holds the edge on repository-wide refactoring (81.3% SWE-bench tier), while GPT-6.2 Sol offers native Responses API background windows.

Frequently asked questions

Is Claude Sonnet 5.5 cheaper than GPT-6.2 Sol?

On list rates, Claude Sonnet 5.5 costs $2.00/$10.00 per 1M input/output tokens versus $2.00/$10.00 for GPT-6.2 Sol. On a 200-step agent workload (40K cached prefix, 500 output tokens per step), Claude Sonnet 5.5 totals $1.90 versus $1.90 for GPT-6.2 Sol. Prices verified November 2026.

Claude Sonnet 5.5 vs GPT-6.2 Sol: which has the longer context window?

Claude Sonnet 5.5 offers 1M tokens; GPT-6.2 Sol offers 1.05M tokens, per provider documentation checked November 2026.

Where do these Claude Sonnet 5.5 and GPT-6.2 Sol prices come from?
