Claude Fable 5.1 vs GPT-6 Astra: Real API Cost Compared

By Meng Guoying (mengg) · Review method · Corrections

The verdict, in numbers: Claude Fable 5.1 and GPT-6 Astra publish identical list rates ($10.00 / $50.00), but identical list rates do not mean identical bills: on our 200-step agent workload the two land 1.8x apart ($7.49 vs $13.46), driven entirely by the cache-read row.

Engineering trade-offs & production reality

Both models share an identical $10/$50 headline rate, but on autonomous agent loops, their billing diverges dramatically. Fable 5.1's $0.25/1M cache read rate is 4x cheaper than Astra's $1.00/1M rate. In a 200-step agent loop where 90%+ of tokens are cached repository context, Fable 5.1 lands at $3.78 per task compared to $6.88 for Astra (a 1.8x difference). Astra counters with native computer-use capabilities and OpenAI's highest cybersecurity preparedness rating. For pure coding agents, Fable's cache math is compelling; for interactive desktop automation, Astra remains the primary contender.

Frequently asked questions

Is Claude Fable 5.1 cheaper than GPT-6 Astra?

On list rates, Claude Fable 5.1 costs $10.00/$50.00 per 1M input/output tokens versus $10.00/$50.00 for GPT-6 Astra. On a 200-step agent workload (40K cached prefix, 500 output tokens per step), Claude Fable 5.1 totals $7.49 versus $13.46 for GPT-6 Astra. Prices verified November 2026.

Claude Fable 5.1 vs GPT-6 Astra: which has the longer context window?
