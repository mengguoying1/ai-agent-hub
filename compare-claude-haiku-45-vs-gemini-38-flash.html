Claude Haiku 4.5 vs Gemini 3.8 Flash: Real API Cost Compared

By Meng Guoying (mengg) · Review method · Corrections

The verdict, in numbers: On our reference 200-step agent workload, Gemini 3.8 Flash bills $0.97 against $1.35 for Claude Haiku 4.5 — 1.4x apart. List rates alone would not have told you that.

Engineering trade-offs & production reality

Fast triage and tool routing: Haiku 4.5 ($1/$5) vs Gemini 3.8 Flash ($0.75/$3.75). Gemini is slightly cheaper on list rates, but Haiku 4.5 exhibits superior prompt following on negative constraints (e.g. 'do NOT edit this file'). For tight CI/CD verification gates where false positives cost developer time, Haiku's precision is often worth the modest premium.

Frequently asked questions

Is Claude Haiku 4.5 cheaper than Gemini 3.8 Flash?

On list rates, Claude Haiku 4.5 costs $1.00/$5.00 per 1M input/output tokens versus $0.75/$3.75 for Gemini 3.8 Flash. On a 200-step agent workload (40K cached prefix, 500 output tokens per step), Gemini 3.8 Flash totals $0.97 versus $1.35 for Claude Haiku 4.5. Prices verified November 2026.

Claude Haiku 4.5 vs Gemini 3.8 Flash: which has the longer context window?
