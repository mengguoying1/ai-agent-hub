Contact & Corrections

Use the structured correction form below, open a public issue on GitHub Issues ↗, or email us directly at contact@waiagent.win.

Email

contact@waiagent.win

Use a descriptive subject such as Data correction, Privacy request, Accessibility issue, or Business inquiry. Do not email passwords, API keys, confidential source code, medical information, or payment data.

Submit a Data Correction or Feedback

Open Public GitHub Issue ↗

Reporting a factual error

  1. Include the exact AI Agent Hub page URL.
  2. Quote the statement or value you believe is wrong.
  3. Provide a primary source, preferably provider documentation.
  4. Explain any relevant region, pricing tier, date, or temporary promotion.

Price, availability, and security corrections receive priority. Accepted changes update the shared data verification date where applicable.

Reporting a technical or accessibility problem

Include the page URL, device/browser, what you expected, what happened, and a screenshot if useful. Please remove personal or confidential information from screenshots.

Commercial inquiries

Advertising or sponsorship proposals are reviewed separately from editorial decisions. Payment does not guarantee coverage, inclusion, ranking, or a favorable conclusion. Any accepted sponsorship or affiliate relationship will be disclosed near the relevant content.

What happens after you write

  1. We confirm the specific claim. A correction is checked against provider documentation before anything changes. Where a provider's own pages disagree, the pricing and billing page is treated as authoritative.
  2. We change the page and the shared data. Rates live in one catalogue file, so a correction propagates to every page that displays it rather than being patched piecemeal.
  3. We record it. Accepted corrections are added to the public correction record with the date, the previous value, and the replacement. Corrections are not applied silently.
  4. We tell you what we concluded. If a report is accepted you will get the change and its date. If it is not accepted, you will get the source we relied on instead.

Corrections that affect a price, a security recommendation, or product availability are handled ahead of wording, formatting, and link issues.

What this site cannot help with

  • Debugging your own integration, or reviewing proprietary code sent by email.
  • Confirming a price that only appears in a sales quote; this site reports published rates.
  • Advice on a specific procurement, legal, or security decision, which depends on your own constraints.
  • Removing published corrections from the record, including ones that reflect poorly on earlier versions of a page.

Privacy requests

Identify the email address or prior message relevant to the request. Review the Privacy Policy for the site's current data practices.
