Contact & Corrections

Email is the site's only current contact channel. There is no web form, account system, guest-post submission portal, or paid-placement checkout.

Email

onenter608@outlook.com

Use a descriptive subject such as Data correction, Privacy request, Accessibility issue, or Business inquiry. Do not email passwords, API keys, confidential source code, medical information, or payment data.

Reporting a factual error

  1. Include the exact AI Agent Hub page URL.
  2. Quote the statement or value you believe is wrong.
  3. Provide a primary source, preferably provider documentation.
  4. Explain any relevant region, pricing tier, date, or temporary promotion.

Price, availability, and security corrections receive priority. Accepted changes update the shared data verification date where applicable.

Reporting a technical or accessibility problem

Include the page URL, device/browser, what you expected, what happened, and a screenshot if useful. Please remove personal or confidential information from screenshots.

Commercial inquiries

Advertising or sponsorship proposals are reviewed separately from editorial decisions. Payment does not guarantee coverage, inclusion, ranking, or a favorable conclusion. Any accepted sponsorship or affiliate relationship will be disclosed near the relevant content.

Privacy requests

Identify the email address or prior message relevant to the request. Review the Privacy Policy for the site's current data practices.
