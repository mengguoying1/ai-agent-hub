By AI Agent Hub Editorial Desk · Review method · Corrections

This page compares list rates and workload cost only. It does not tell you which model is better — capability belongs to your own evaluation. What it does give you is the half of the decision that can be verified: what each provider will actually charge, read from their own documentation on September 18, 2026.

Frequently asked questions

Is Claude Opus 5 cheaper than GPT-5.6 Sol?

On list rates, Claude Opus 5 costs $5.00/$25.00 per 1M input/output tokens versus $4.00/$20.00 for GPT-5.6 Sol. On a 200-step agent workload (40K cached prefix, 500 output tokens per step), GPT-5.6 Sol totals $5.38 versus $6.73 for Claude Opus 5. Prices verified September 18, 2026.

Claude Opus 5 vs GPT-5.6 Sol: which has the longer context window?

Claude Opus 5 offers 1M tokens; GPT-5.6 Sol offers 1.05M tokens, per provider documentation checked September 18, 2026.

Where do these Claude Opus 5 and GPT-5.6 Sol prices come from?

Both rate cards were read from the providers' own pricing pages on September 18, 2026: https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/pricing and https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models/gpt-5.6-sol.
