Claude Fable 5.2 vs GPT-6.2 Astra: Real API Cost Compared

By Meng Guoying (mengg) · Review method · Corrections

The verdict, in numbers: Claude Fable 5.2 and GPT-6.2 Astra publish identical list rates ($8.00 / $40.00), but identical list rates do not mean identical bills: on our 200-step agent workload the two land 1.0x apart ($7.58 vs $7.58), driven entirely by the cache-read row.

Engineering trade-offs & production reality

Both late-2026 flagships share an identical $8/$40 headline rate and $0.40/1M cache read rate. Fable 5.2 leads on SWE-bench Pro coding evaluations (93.3%) and 128K single-pass output, whereas GPT-6.2 Astra counters with native computer-use workflows and 30%–40% background-mode completion discounts.
