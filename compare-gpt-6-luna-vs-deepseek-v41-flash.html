GPT-6 Luna vs DeepSeek V4.1 Flash: Real API Cost Compared

By Meng Guoying (mengg) · Review method · Corrections

The verdict, in numbers: On our reference 200-step agent workload, DeepSeek V4.1 Flash bills $0.09 against $0.13 for GPT-6 Luna — 1.5x apart. List rates alone would not have told you that.

Engineering trade-offs & production reality

GPT-6 Luna ($0.10/$0.50) now undercuts even DeepSeek V4.1 Flash's off-peak base rate ($0.15/$0.60), though DeepSeek retains an edge on cached reads ($0.003 vs $0.01) and 384K max output.

Frequently asked questions

Is GPT-6 Luna cheaper than DeepSeek V4.1 Flash?

On list rates, GPT-6 Luna costs $0.10/$0.50 per 1M input/output tokens versus $0.15/$0.60 for DeepSeek V4.1 Flash. On a 200-step agent workload (40K cached prefix, 500 output tokens per step), DeepSeek V4.1 Flash totals $0.09 versus $0.13 for GPT-6 Luna. Prices verified November 2026.

GPT-6 Luna vs DeepSeek V4.1 Flash: which has the longer context window?

GPT-6 Luna offers 1.05M tokens; DeepSeek V4.1 Flash offers 1M tokens, per provider documentation checked November 2026.

Where do these GPT-6 Luna and DeepSeek V4.1 Flash prices come from?

Both rate cards were read from the providers' own pricing pages in November 2026: https://openai.com/api/pricing/ and https://api-docs.deepseek.com/quick_start/pricing/.
